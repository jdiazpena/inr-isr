# Public AMISR experiments with at least 40 beam directions

This list documents 15 public AMISR experiment records identified through preliminary archive screening. Each experiment contains at least 40 beam directions and therefore provides a candidate multi-beam data set for the proposed continuous four-dimensional reconstruction study.

Beam count establishes the initial multi-beam screening criterion. Final scientific use will be determined by the event-specific availability and adequacy of the radar products and supporting physical information required by the project.
